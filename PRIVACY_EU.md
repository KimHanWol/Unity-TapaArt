# Tapa Sketchbook Privacy Notice for the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This notice applies to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. It adds to the general [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) and explains how your data is handled under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

한국어는 아래에 있습니다.

## 1. Who is responsible
The data controller for the app is the developer of Tapa Sketchbook, published on GitHub as KimHanWol.
Contact: https://github.com/KimHanWol/Unity-TapaSketch/issues

For personal data collected through ads, Google is an independent controller. In the EEA and Switzerland this is Google Ireland Limited, and in the UK Google LLC.

## 2. What is processed, why, and on what legal basis

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game progress and settings, stored only on your device | Running the game | Not sent to the developer or any server |
| Your ad consent choice, stored on your device | Remembering your choice | Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, processed by Google AdMob and its ad partners | Personalized ads and ad measurement | Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| The same kinds of data, processed by Google AdMob | Non-personalized ads, security and fraud prevention | Your consent where required for device access, otherwise legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in funding the free game and preventing abuse |
| IP address and request details, received by GitHub when the app checks for a new version at startup | Telling you about updates | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the app working and secure |

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. The developer does not receive the advertising data above in a form that identifies you.

## 3. Your consent
On first launch, the app shows a consent message from Google in regions where consent is required. You can accept or decline. Declining does not stop you from playing, but you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

You can withdraw your consent at any time. To do this, clear the app's storage in your device settings or reinstall the app, and the consent message appears again on the next launch. This also resets your game progress on that device. Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew.

You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

## 4. Who receives the data
- Google and the ad technology partners named in the consent message
- GitHub, Inc., which hosts the version file

How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## 5. Transfers outside your region
Google and GitHub may process data outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, including in the United States. These companies state that they rely on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 6. How long data is kept
- Game progress, settings and your consent choice stay on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
- Google and GitHub keep data according to their own retention policies, linked above.

## 7. Your rights
You may have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because the developer holds no data that identifies you, most requests about ad data are best sent to Google: https://support.google.com/policies/contact/general_privacy_form

You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

## 8. Profiling
If you consent to personalized ads, Google and its partners may use automated processing to choose which ads you see. This has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

## 9. Children
The app is not directed to children under 13. Where your country sets a higher age for consenting to online services (up to 16), a parent or guardian must give consent.

## 10. Changes
If this notice changes, the updated notice and its effective date will be posted on this page.

## 3. 동의
동의가 필요한 지역에서는 앱을 처음 실행할 때 Google의 동의 메시지가 나옵니다. 동의하거나 거부할 수 있습니다. 거부해도 게임은 할 수 있으며, 비맞춤 광고나 제한된 광고가 나올 수 있습니다.

동의는 언제든 철회할 수 있습니다. 기기 설정에서 앱 저장공간을 지우거나 앱을 다시 설치하면 다음 실행 때 동의 메시지가 다시 나옵니다. 이때 그 기기의 게임 진행 상황도 함께 초기화됩니다. 철회해도 철회 전에 이루어진 처리에는 영향이 없습니다.

광고 ID는 기기 설정에서 삭제하거나 재설정할 수 있습니다.
